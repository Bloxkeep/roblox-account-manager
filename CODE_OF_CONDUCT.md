# Code of conduct

Bloxkeep's GitHub pages, its [Discord server](https://bloxkeep-bot.bloxkeep-app.workers.dev/discord) and anywhere else
people talk about it should be places where anyone can ask a question or report a problem without being put down.

## What we expect

- Be kind and patient, also with people who are new to Roblox tools or who don't write English well.
- Disagree with ideas, not with people. Keep to the topic.
- Respect privacy: never post anyone's cookie, password, 2-step code, account name or personal details, yours included.

## What isn't accepted

- Harassment, insults, slurs, threats, or sexual content.
- Posting someone's personal information without their permission.
- Scams: fake "Multi Roblox" downloads or extensions, cookie loggers, or asking anyone for their cookie or password.
- Promoting cheats or exploits that break Roblox's Terms of Use.
- Spam and unrelated advertising.

## Reporting

- **On GitHub**: use **Report content** in the comment's **⋯** menu, or open an issue if it is about the project itself.
- **On Discord**: send the server's admins a direct message, or use Discord's own **Report**.

Reports are handled privately. The maintainer may edit or remove posts, lock threads, or block or ban someone,
depending on what happened.

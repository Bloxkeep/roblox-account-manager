# How to run multiple Roblox accounts at once on Windows

Roblox normally allows one window on a PC: opening a second one closes the first. To play on several accounts at once, turn on Multi-Roblox in Bloxkeep, a free, open-source account manager for Windows 10 and 11, then select your accounts and press Play. Each account opens in its own window.

## Quick steps

1. [Download Bloxkeep](https://github.com/Bloxkeep/roblox-account-manager/releases/latest), run `Bloxkeep-Setup.exe`, and set a master password.
2. Click **Add account** and sign in to each Roblox account.
3. If Roblox is already open, press **Close all Roblox**. Then turn on the switch on the **Multi-Roblox** card at the bottom of the sidebar.
4. Check that the light is green and the card says **On — several windows allowed**.
5. Pick a game, select your accounts and press **Play**. Keep Bloxkeep open while you play (the tray is fine).

<img src="../screenshots/hero.png" width="760" alt="Bloxkeep's main window: account cards, the game bar at the bottom, Multi-Roblox on in the sidebar">

> [!WARNING]
> Roblox doesn't officially support running several clients, so use Multi-Roblox at your own risk. Some games also have their own rules about alt accounts.

## Questions

### **Q:** Is Bloxkeep free?

**A:** Yes. It is free and open source under the MIT license.

### **Q:** Does it inject anything into Roblox?

**A:** No. Roblox uses a lock to allow only one window, and Bloxkeep holds that lock's name first, while no Roblox is running. [Multi-Roblox](multi-roblox.md) explains it.

### **Q:** Can I get banned for this?

**A:** Nobody can promise you won't. Roblox doesn't officially support several clients, and some games forbid alt accounts, so check the rules of the game you play.

### **Q:** How many accounts can I run?

**A:** It depends on your PC and the game, because each window needs RAM of its own and a heavy game can hold several GB. Start with a few and add more. Bloxkeep can warn you when RAM runs low.

### **Q:** Where are my accounts kept?

**A:** On your PC, encrypted with your master password. [Backup and security](backup-and-security.md) has the details.

### **Q:** The windows close each other. What now?

**A:** Close every Roblox, including the one in the system tray, then turn on Multi-Roblox again. See [Fixing "Not working yet"](multi-roblox.md#fixing-not-working-yet).

### **Q:** Does it work on Mac or on a phone?

**A:** No. Bloxkeep runs on Windows 10 or 11, 64-bit.

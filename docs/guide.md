# Bloxkeep user guide

Short pages, one per topic. Read the first three to get going; open the others when you need them.

<img src="screenshots/hero.png" width="760" alt="Bloxkeep's main window: account cards, the game bar at the bottom, Multi-Roblox on in the sidebar">

## Start here

1. [Install Bloxkeep and set your master password](guide/getting-started.md)
2. [Add your Roblox accounts](guide/accounts.md)
3. [Pick a game and play](guide/playing.md)

> [!TIP]
> Playing on several accounts at once? [Run multiple accounts](guide/run-multiple-accounts.md) shows the whole way, with [Multi-Roblox](guide/multi-roblox.md) turned on first. Press **Ctrl+K** in the app to find any action or setting by name.
